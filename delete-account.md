# Delete your SceneNest account

This page explains how to request deletion of your **SceneNest** account and the data held with
it. SceneNest is published by **VSJ Technology**.

You only have an account if you chose to sign in. SceneNest works fully offline without one, and
if you never signed in there is no account to delete — see [What stays on your
device](#what-stays-on-your-device) below.

## How to request deletion

1. Email **[scenenest.privacy@outlook.com](mailto:scenenest.privacy@outlook.com)** from the email
   address you use to sign in to SceneNest.
2. Put **Delete my account** in the subject line.
3. If you sign in with Google or Apple, say which — your SceneNest account is identified by that
   email address.

Sending from your own sign-in address is what lets us confirm the request is yours. If you cannot
send from that address, say so in your message and we will ask you for another way to confirm it.

**We will confirm by email when the deletion is complete, within 30 days of your request.**

There is currently no way to delete your account from inside the app. The email route above is the
only one, and it is the request we act on.

## What is deleted

Everything below is deleted permanently and cannot be recovered:

- **Your sign-in account** — held with Microsoft Entra External ID, including your email address
  and the display name you chose at sign-up.
- **Your SceneNest profile** — display name, email address and profile-picture link.
- **Your synced watchlists** — every list and saved title held in your account, and the sync
  history used to keep your devices in step.
- **Family Sharing** — your membership of any group, and any list you shared with one. If you own
  a group, the group itself is deleted and the other members lose access to lists you shared.

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Operational records** — records of requests made to the SceneNest service, kept for **30 days**
  and then deleted automatically. These identify a device and network address only as a one-way
  hash, never your name, email or account, and they are not linked back to you after your account
  is gone.
- **Nothing else.** SceneNest does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising or
  profiling, and keeps no advertising or analytics profile to delete.

Signing out is **not** deletion: it ends the session on that device and stops syncing, but leaves
your account and the copy of your lists in it untouched.

## What stays on your device

Your device keeps its own copy of your lists, so that SceneNest works offline. Deleting your
account does not erase that copy. To remove it, delete the items in the app or uninstall
SceneNest — uninstalling removes all local SceneNest data from the device.
